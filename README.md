## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

Farm2Market is an AI-powered digital marketplace designed to connect farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers. It helps farmers sell their agricultural products at fair prices without depending on multiple middlemen.

The platform provides the following features:
- Direct Marketplace: Farmers can upload their products, quantity, quality and expected prices.
- AI Price Prediction: Suggests suitable selling prices based on market demand and product availability.
- Smart Buyer Matching: Connects farmers with nearby consumers, supermarkets, hotels and wholesale buyers.
- Logistics Support: Helps arrange transportation and delivery from farms to customers.
- Demand Forecasting: Uses AI to predict future demand for agricultural products.
- Multilingual Support: Allows farmers to use the application in Tamil, English and other regional languages.

How it addresses the problem
- Reduces the number of intermediaries.
- Helps farmers receive better prices.
- Makes agricultural products more affordable for consumers.
- Reduces delays in transportation and delivery.

Innovation and uniqueness

Our solution combines AI-based price prediction, demand forecasting, direct purchasing and smart logistics in one platform. It also provides regional language support to make technology accessible to rural farmers.

## Feasibility and Viability

Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
- The project can be developed using affordable and easily accessible technologies.
- A basic prototype can be created using Python, HTML, CSS and MySQL.
- Farmers and buyers can access the platform through smartphones or computers.
- The application can initially be tested with a limited number of farmers and products.

Potential challenges and risks
1. Lack of digital awareness among some farmers.
2. Transportation and delivery difficulties.
3. Fluctuations in agricultural product prices.
4. Internet connectivity problems in rural areas.
5. Maintaining product quality during transportation.

Strategies for overcoming these challenges
- Provide a simple interface with regional language support.
- Offer easy tutorials and guidance for farmers.
- Connect with local transport providers.
- Use AI-based market analysis to suggest reasonable prices.
- Introduce product quality verification and buyer feedback.
- Support low-bandwidth access and basic offline features.
